# LuxeFuel Feature Roadmap

This document outlines the strategic feature expansion for LuxeFuel across its three subscription tiers.

## 1. Free Tier (The "Initiate" Level)
*Objective: Increase daily app engagement through social and predictive features.*

- **Next-Burn Forecast:** Predicts the exact date the fuel tank will hit 0% based on average daily distance. Includes a "Days Remaining" countdown.
- **Neon Garage Customization:** Personalize vehicles with custom "Callsigns" and signature UI accent colors.
- **Social Achievement Sharing:** Export high-contrast achievement cards (e.g., *Ghost Driver*) to social media.

## 2. Pro Tier (The "Eco-Elite" Level)
*Objective: Provide deep technical insights for power users and machine masters.*

- **Fuel-Grade Efficiency Matrix:** Compare performance (km/L) between different fuel types (e.g., Gasohol 95 vs E20) to find the best power-to-cost ratio.
- **Smart Service Reminders:** Automatic integration with system calendars when vehicle health scores drop below 20%.
- **Regional "Optimal-Stop" Map:** Highlight the nearest stations with the lowest prices using live Thai Fuel Index data.

## 3. Enterprise Tier (The "Command Center" Level)
*Objective: reduce organizational overhead and automate fleet oversight.*

- **AI Driver Coaching:** Automated performance briefs that provide actionable advice to improve driver `Eco-Pulse` scores.
- **Geofenced Audit Alerts:** Real-time alerts for fueling sessions logged at prices significantly higher than the market average.
- **Financial Ops Dashboard:** Automated tracking of VAT/Tax recovery on fuel expenses with standard accounting software exports.
